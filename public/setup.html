Connect Snowflake

  1. In Snowflake, open Projects → Worksheets and make a new SQL worksheet.
  2. Paste this line, replacing the name with your Snowflake username if it's wrong, and press Run: 
    loading…
  3. On the server, add these two lines to /root/pause/.env: 
    SNOWFLAKE_ACCOUNT=your-account-identifier
SNOWFLAKE_USER=your-username
    The account identifier is under your name (bottom left) → Account → Copy account identifier. It looks like ABCDEFG-XY12345.
  4. Run pm2 restart pause --update-env and refresh this page.
