How Undo works

A cooling-off period between Grandma and the scammer.

  1. The money waits.A big payment goes into a vault on Solana for 24 hours instead of leaving right away.
  2. The AI explains it.Undo reads the message that asked for money, finds the red flags, and tells her in plain, kind words.
  3. Family gets a text.One tap cancels it and the money goes straight back to her. If it's really Jake, one tap approves it.
